# Start Here: AXIS-NIDDHI in 3 Minutes

[English](START_HERE.md) · [Español](START_HERE.es-419.md) · [Italiano](START_HERE.it.md) · [Français](START_HERE.fr.md) · [Português](START_HERE.pt-BR.md)

AXIS-NIDDHI is a preservation and translation project for the teaching corpus at PureDhamma.net. It keeps the corpus readable as a static archive and supports careful comparison with the English source.

## What you will find

- A static archive that can also be distributed for offline reading.
- Translations supported by terminology glossaries and human review.
- A traceable publishing process, with source comparisons and release records.

PureDhamma.net remains the primary reference. AXIS-NIDDHI does not replace it or offer a new interpretation of Dhamma. A published translation is not automatically final or doctrinally certified.

## How to use the archive

1. Open the public site and enter the archive.
2. Choose a section or search for a topic or **PD#PN**.
3. Select a language. English is always the reference language.
4. Compare the translation with English when available; read any status notice.
5. Give preservation of meaning priority over polished wording.

Public sites: [Netlify](https://niddhi.netlify.app/) and [Cloudflare review preview](https://niddhi.pages.dev/welcome?lang=en). Their release stages may differ.

## Languages and translation status

The interface offers English, Español, Italiano, Français and Português. Article availability varies: Portuguese and Spanish include study translations; Italian and French navigation can still lead to pages awaiting translation. A language button does not guarantee a translated article.

Translations support study and review for readers who are not fluent in English. They are not official, final or doctrinally certified. The English articles at PureDhamma.net remain the primary reference. Report unclear, unusual or contradictory passages as review points.

## Reading tools

Red words have pronunciation recordings. Click or tap to listen. Amber words are glossary terms without a recording; hover, focus or tap for an explanation. Theme controls adjust reading appearance.

LABZ offers optional exploration, artwork by Mariinha and a browser-local reading list. Random discovery is not a prescribed study sequence. Local notes and lists are not shared automatically.

## How to help

- Report a confusing passage with its **PD#PN**, language and section.
- Compare the translation with the English source.
- Identify Pāḷi terms that need more careful handling.
- Suggest clearer documentation or report an accessibility problem.

Read the [translation review guide](TRANSLATION_REVIEW_GUIDE.md) in English. Aim for a precise improvement that preserves the meaning.
